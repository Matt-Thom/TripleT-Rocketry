**Executive Summary**

NAR/TRA motor impulse classes A–O are defined by total impulse (N·s) ranges that double at each step. Standard model rocketry (NFPA 1122) covers up to G; high-power rocketry (HPR, NFPA 1127) begins at H. NAR/TRA certification levels map as Level 1 (H–I), Level 2 (J–L), and Level 3 (M–O). These facts are suitable for direct inclusion in an ADR.

**Key Findings (with citations)**

- Impulse classes and ranges (N·s) are standardized and identical across NAR, TRA, and NFPA references [1]:
  - A: 1.26–2.50
  - B: 2.51–5.00
  - C: 5.01–10.0
  - D: 10.01–20.0
  - E: 20.01–40.0
  - F: 40.01–80.0
  - G: 80.01–160
  - H: 160.01–320
  - I: 320.01–640
  - J: 640.01–1,280
  - K: 1,280.01–2,560
  - L: 2,560.01–5,120
  - M: 5,120.01–10,240
  - N: 10,240.01–20,480
  - O: 20,480.01–40,960

- Certification thresholds (NAR/TRA) [1]:
  - Level 1: H–I
  - Level 2: J–L
  - Level 3: M–O

- Regulatory split: motors ≤ G are “model rocket” (NFPA 1122); motors ≥ H are “high-power rocket” (NFPA 1127) and require the corresponding certification level plus range-safety oversight [1].
