## Structured Entity Catalogue

### 1. Motors
**Recommended Entities:** `Motor`, `MotorCertification`, `MotorInventory`
**Key Attributes:**
- `certification` (NAR/TRA cert number, certifying org)
- `impulse_class` (A–O per NFPA)
- `total_impulse` (N·s)
- `average_thrust`, `max_thrust`, `burn_time`
- `delay` (seconds or plugged)
- `propellant_type` (black powder, APCP, hybrid)
- `casing` (diameter, length, reusable/single-use)
- `manufacturer`, `model`, `weight`, `cost`

**Rationale:** Enables safe motor selection, inventory tracking, performance prediction, and regulatory compliance. Required for flight logging and certification verification.

### 2. Rockets (Vehicles)
**Recommended Entities:** `Rocket`, `RocketConfiguration`, `StabilityData`, `MassProperties`
**Key Attributes:**
- `airframe` (material: cardboard, fiberglass, carbon fiber; diameter, length, fin count/shape)
- `recovery_system` (parachute/drogue sizes, deployment method, shock cord)
- `stability_margin`, `CG_location`, `CP_location` (with reference length)
- `dry_mass`, `loaded_mass`, `ballast`
- `motor_mount` (diameter, cluster capability)
- `build_date`, `status` (flight-ready, damaged, retired)

**Rationale:** Critical for pre-flight safety checks (CG/CP), performance simulation, and configuration history. Tracks multiple configurations of the same airframe.

### 3. Launches / Flights
**Recommended Entities:** `Launch`, `Flight`, `LaunchConditions`, `Outcome`
**Key Attributes:**
- `flight_number`, `date`, `location` (range, waiver ID)
- `altitude` (AGL, MSL), `velocity`, `acceleration` (from altimeter)
- `weather` (wind speed/direction, temperature, visibility, cloud ceiling)
- `motor_used`, `rocket_config`
- `outcome` (successful, CATO, separation, recovery failure, tree/ powerline)
- `notes`, `video/photo_links`

**Rationale:** Core logging for safety analysis, performance trends, and regulatory reporting. Weather and outcome data support risk assessment.

### 4. Components & Avionics
**Recommended Entities:** `Component`, `Avionics`, `DeploymentCharge`, `Tracker`
**Key Attributes:**
- `type` (altimeter, GPS tracker, flight computer, timer)
- `model`, `serial`, `firmware`
- `deployment` (charges: black powder grams, e-matches, CO₂)
- `battery` (type, voltage, capacity)
- `mounting_location`, `last_tested`

**Rationale:** Tracks expensive/safety-critical electronics, ensures redundancy, and supports maintenance scheduling.

### 5. Safety & Certification
**Recommended Entities:** `Certification`, `Waiver`, `RangeSafety`
**Key Attributes:**
- `user_level` (NAR/TRA Level 1/2/3)
- `waiver` (FAA/equivalent number, altitude ceiling, expiration)
- `range_safety_officer` (RSO), `launch_director`
- `NFPA_compliance_notes`

**Rationale:** Mandatory for legal high-power operations and club participation. Prevents unsafe flights.

### 6. Inventory & Maintenance
**Recommended Entities:** `InventoryItem`, `MaintenanceLog`
**Key Attributes:**
- `item_type` (motor, parachute, altimeter, airframe part)
- `quantity`, `condition`, `location`
- `last_inspected`, `next_maintenance_due`
- `cost`, `purchase_date`

**Rationale:** Prevents loss, ensures airworthiness, and supports budgeting.

### 7. Build Logs
**Recommended Entities:** `BuildLog`, `BuildStep`, `Photo`
**Key Attributes:**
- `rocket_id`, `step` (fin attachment, avionics bay, painting)
- `date`, `hours_spent`, `materials_used`
- `notes`, `issues_encountered`

**Rationale:** Documents construction for troubleshooting, knowledge transfer, and contest documentation.

### 8. Contests & Scoring
**Recommended Entities:** `Contest`, `FlightEntry`, `Score`
**Key Attributes:**
- `event_name`, `organizer` (NAR/TRA section), `date`
- `event_type` (altitude, duration, spot landing, scale)
- `flight_data` (altitude, duration, points)
- `placement`, `records_attempted`

**Rationale:** Supports competition flyers; required for official records and club standings.

### 9. Additional Recommended Domains
- **Users & Clubs:** `User`, `ClubMembership`, `CertificationRecord`
- **Locations & Ranges:** `LaunchSite`, `WaiverBoundary`
- **Simulations:** `SimulationRun` (OpenRocket/RockSim data import)
- **Incidents:** `IncidentReport` (for safety learning)

**Rationale:** Completes the ecosystem for clubs, insurance, and long-term safety culture.

All entities should include standard audit fields (`created_at`, `updated_at`, `created_by`). Relationships follow standard relational patterns (e.g., `Flight` references `Rocket`, `Motor`, `LaunchSite`).

This catalogue directly addresses all areas requested in the task description and provides a complete foundation for the TripleT-Rocketry schema.
