NFPA 1122 is the Code for Model Rocketry (motors ≤G / model rockets). NFPA 1127 is the Code for High Power Rocketry (motors ≥H, typically ≥160.1 N·s total impulse and/or other HPR mass/propellant/thrust thresholds). Current editions 2026; next 2030. Official NFPA pages: nfpa.org 1122 and 1127 standard-development. HPR also defined as total weight >1500 g and/or propellant >125 g and/or average thrust ≥80 N.
