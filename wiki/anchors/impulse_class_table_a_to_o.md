NAR/TRA/NFPA consumer-rocket impulse classes A–O (total impulse N·s; each letter doubles the previous upper bound): A 1.26–2.50; B 2.51–5.00; C 5.01–10.0; D 10.01–20.0; E 20.01–40.0; F 40.01–80.0; G 80.01–160; H 160.01–320; I 320.01–640; J 640.01–1,280; K 1,280.01–2,560; L 2,560.01–5,120; M 5,120.01–10,240; N 10,240.01–20,480; O 20,480.01–40,960. H lower bound is also cited as 160.1 N·s in NFPA/HPR summaries. Sources: Wikipedia Model rocket motor classification; Wikipedia High-power rocketry; prior QRY-dlg-730f914f467b.
