NAR/TRA high-power certification (US): Level 1 = H–I; Level 2 = J–L; Level 3 = M–O. Reciprocal recognition between NAR and TRA. Canada (CAR) inserts extra steps (L1=H, L2=I, L3=US L2, L4=US L3). UKRA/Rocketry SA match US L1–L3 through O. TripleT-Rocketry must treat this as a soft-gate lookup for Motor/Certification/Flight — warnings + audit only, no hard regulatory engine.
